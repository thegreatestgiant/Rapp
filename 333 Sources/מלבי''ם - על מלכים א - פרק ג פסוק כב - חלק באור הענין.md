### מלבי''ם - על מלכים א - פרק ג פסוק כב - חלק באור הענין
You talk about what you care about.
The mother said mine is the live one, and the other's is the dead one
The liar said the others is the dead one and the live one is mine
Then שלמה repeated this to prove his point
