### חכם צבי - חכם צבי - סימן מ''ט

How can you get punished for everything except for כרת? That makes no sense.
Every single day a boy doesn't have a bris, he is over an עשה. He only gets כרת when he dies. Even if he died at 18.
What about [[תלמוד בבלי - מסכת שבת - פט ב#תלמוד בבלי - מסכת שבת - פט ב]]? It's an aggaditah.
The sons of Yehuda died under 20, when they sinned with תמר
Maybe the כרת waits until you are 20
