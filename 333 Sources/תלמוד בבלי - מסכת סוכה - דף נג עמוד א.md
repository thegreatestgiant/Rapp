### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת סוכה - דף נג עמוד א
There were 2 black people by שלמה, and the Angel of Death said they would die today, so שלמה summoned up some angels to take them to the city of Luz (immortal city)
They were about to enter Luz, and then they collapse dead.
The Angel of Death starts laughing because they were supposed to die at the gates of Luz
