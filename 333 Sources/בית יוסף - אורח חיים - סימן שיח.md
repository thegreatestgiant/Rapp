### בית יוסף - אורח חיים - סימן שיח

The ר'ן says that you can scoop food off of inedible food on the fire
This is for food, and not dying wool
There are people who say the only issue with [[מגיס|Stirring]] is the first part
The [[רמב''ם]] says there is an issue of removing a fork from the cholent because it might [[מגיס|Stir]]
Once it's been completely cooked, you don't need to stir to speed up the cooking
