The word לאמר is said twice in this 1 פסוק. Why? What does it mean?
You'll only have a מלכות בית דוד if...you keep the תורה.
There will still be a מלכות בית דוד. It will always exist.
Ok, so what will it rule over? Either all of Israel or just ירושלים
This explains both לאמר's
