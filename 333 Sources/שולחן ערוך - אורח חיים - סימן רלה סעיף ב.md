### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן רלה סעיף ב
You must stop eating 1/2 hour before Maariv. What if you are in middle of a meal? Stop, say קריאת שמע, and continue with your meal
### [[רמ''א]]
You only stop for קריאת שמע, but only if you had started eating food
