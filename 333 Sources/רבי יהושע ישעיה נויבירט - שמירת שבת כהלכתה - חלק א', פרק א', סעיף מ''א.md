### רבי יהושע ישעיה נויבירט - שמירת שבת כהלכתה - חלק א', פרק א', סעיף מ''א
Even when it isn't on the fire, there might be a problem with covering

If it isn't cooked, it's a חומרה to not put it back on.
You can eat it, but don't do it.

When putting the cover back on, make sure it's dry.
You can put it back on by taking it off of the fire
