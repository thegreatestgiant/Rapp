### פרקי דרבי אליעזר - חורב - פרק יז
- **Prophecy of יאשיהו by Name (1 Kings 13:2):**
  - The איש אלקים prophesies: *"הנה בן נולד לבית דוד יאשיהו שמו"*—foretelling by name that a king of the Davidic line named יאשיהו will shatter this altar and burn the idolatrous priests' bones upon it.
  - **300 Years in Advance:** ר' נתנאל emphasizes that יאשיהו was named **300 years before he was born** (one of the select few individuals in Tanakh named prior to birth, alongside יצחק, ישמעאל, משה, שלמה, and משיח).
- **The Wonder of King יאשיהו's Mindset:**
  - He became king when he was only an 8-year-old child (*נער של שמנה שנים מלך*).
  - How could an 8-year-old boy have the mature understanding to lead a massive spiritual revolution? Despite his youth, he contemplated in his heart, rejected idolatry, sought Hashem, and ultimately fulfilled this exact 300-year-old prophecy by destroying the altar of בית אל (2 Kings 23).
