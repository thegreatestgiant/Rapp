### תנ''ך - זכריה - פרק ח פסוק יט
The 4 fasts (17 Tammuz, 9 Av, Tzom Gedalia, 10 Tevet) will eventually turn into days of joy and feasting (ששון ושמחה).
This is the פסוק the גמ in ר''ה quotes to say when there is peace they become ימים טובים, and when there's no peace they are fasts.
