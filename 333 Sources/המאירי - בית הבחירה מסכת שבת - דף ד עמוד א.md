### המאירי - בית הבחירה מסכת שבת - דף ד עמוד א
- **Baking vs. Scraping**: Baking is an אב מלאכה (דאורייתא). Scraping bread out (רדיית הפת) is only a שבות (דרבנן) because "חכמה היא ואינה מלאכה".
- **במזיד (intentional) $\rightarrow$ מותר to scrape it out**:
  - Allowed to violate a שבות before crust forms (קודם שיקרמו פניה) to avoid the severe penalty of סקילה.
  - *Warning (התראה)*: Valid even though it's an התראת ספק (since he could claim he'd remove it), because either התראת ספק counts once completed, or it's considered התראת ודאי since the oven bakes on its own.
  - Even if removing raw dough has "קצת מלאכה", it's permitted to avoid סקילה.
- **בשוגג (unintentional) $\rightarrow$ אסור to scrape it out**:
  - *If he realizes before crust forms*: There is no longer a חיוב חטאת anyway (awareness before completion exempts him from a korban), so we don't permit violating a שבות.
  - *If it already crusted*: He is already liable; removing it now changes nothing, and the bread is מעשה שבת.
