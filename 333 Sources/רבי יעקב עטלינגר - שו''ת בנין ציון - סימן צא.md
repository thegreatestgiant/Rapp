### רבי יעקב עטלינגר - שו''ת בנין ציון - סימן צא
In 1848 on a tuesday, in morocco a non-jew came for Gairus. By Friday he was feeling so well after the מילה, so he didn't go in the mikva. The question was if he has to do Melacha, that he needs to do, on שבת.
They sent it to the Head Rabbi of Yerushalayim, but he was out and his sub-in (Rav Asher Lemel), said he's obligated to break שבת because he is not a jew yet.
All the Rabbi's who heard about this thought this was crazy.
So the sub sent out letter to [[רבי יעקב עטלינגר]] and then got thrown under the bus. He should not have violated shabbos. After the Milah, he is considered a בן ברית and שבת is another ברית

They did a ברית when they left Egypt was for the sake of conversion.
A half-Ger is not only allowed, but he might be obligated
