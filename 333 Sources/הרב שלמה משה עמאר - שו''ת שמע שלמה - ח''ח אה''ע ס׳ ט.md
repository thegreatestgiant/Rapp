### הרב שלמה משה עמאר - שו''ת שמע שלמה - ח''ח אה''ע ס׳ ט
The sperm takes time to inseminate the egg. Up to 3 days.
All we care about is the act, and not the result.
If you plant now we stone you even though it won't take root for 3 days.
The [[רש''ש]] says that you can pick it back up and it won't be an issue.

Everyone agrees the איסור is from the beginning, even though the child doesn't happen until later.
