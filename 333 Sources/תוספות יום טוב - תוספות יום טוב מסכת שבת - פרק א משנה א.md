### תוספות יום טוב - תוספות יום טוב מסכת שבת - פרק א משנה א

What's the idea here? Later we have a story.
2 babies are born, one friday and one on shabbos, but the moel messes up and gives the shabbos baby a ברית on friday and does the friday baby on שבת, which isn't allowed. But the משנה says it's ok because he was doing a מצוה.
Summary: The משנה seems to be saying it's ok to do the עבירה because he was doing a מצוה at the time
So if a מצוה causes me to do an עבירה I'm [[פטור אבל אסור]]. 
Q: So why is צדקה different? It's because צדקה is never דוחה שבת, but the others are.
