### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת ראש השנה - דף יח עמוד ב דף יט עמוד ב
Dispute if [[מגילת תענית]] was cancelled after the חורבן:
- רב and ר' חנינא: It's totally cancelled. When there is peace (the ב''ה stands), fasts turn to holidays. When there's no peace, they are fasts. And all the other minor holidays in מגילת תענית are cancelled too.
- ר' יוחנן and ר' יהושע בן לוי: Only the 4 fasts that were tied to the ב''ה were changed, but the other holidays in מגילת תענית still stand!
The גמ asks we have two contradictory הלכות: one says it's cancelled, one says not!
Resolution: Not a contradiction!
- חנוכה and פורים are NEVER cancelled, they stay forever!
- All other minor holidays in מגילת תענית were cancelled.

Big question: Why are חנוכה and פורים different than every other holiday in מגילת תענית? Why did they survive the חורבן?
