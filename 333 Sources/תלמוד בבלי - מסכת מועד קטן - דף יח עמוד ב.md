### גמרא/ אמר שמואל
We know that you can't get married on חול המועד
Can you get engaged? Sure. Make sure that no one else will get her. But wait, aren't we destined for each other (בשערט)? No, we think someone will daven and change things
Rava once heard someone Daven that a women should be convinced to marry him. Rava said you shouldn't daven that way, if she is your [[בשערט]] then you don't need to pray, and if she isn't then you don't want her. Then he later found him davening that one of them should die
