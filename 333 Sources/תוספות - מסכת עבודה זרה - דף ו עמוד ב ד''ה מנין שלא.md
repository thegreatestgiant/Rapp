### מנין שלא יושיט אדם כוס יין לנזיר
You can't cause anyone to do anything אסור
Maybe when someone asks you to pass something, they don't intend to eat it, or they don't realize it.
You cannot pass to non-religious or non-Jew even if they can get it anyways. But only when they can't do it themselves
