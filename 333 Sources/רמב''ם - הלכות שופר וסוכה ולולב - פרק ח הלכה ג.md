### רמב''ם - הלכות שופר וסוכה ולולב - פרק ח הלכה ג
It depends on how far apart the leaves are.
- Are they completely off the spine
- Are they slightly spread off

#### [[ראב''ד]]
When a palm leaf spreads out 2 things happen
1. It spreads away from the spine
2. It starts blooming/opening
