### אגרות משה - חלק או''ח ד - סימן מט
Women can keep whatever מצוה they want, and they get שכר for it, and according to תוס they can even make ברכות.
Don't only men wear ציצית so it's בגד איש? So make a girl בגד.
Women can't wear תפילין.
When it says you can't cross-dress it says women can't wear ציצית ותפילין.
The only reason they would be allowed to do it, would be if they had a strong spiritual drive to do so. 
But if it's just to make a statement, which is most people who want to do it, it's כפירה
