### מגן אברהם - אורח חיים - סימן תקפח ס''ק ד
Why do we talk about carrying? Blowing a Musical instrument like a Shofar is in itself אסור.
Not blowing is דרבנן, and Blowing is דאורייתא, so you would have to blow.
