### והאדם הישר העובד אמיתי
The תורה is the blueprint of the world. You can therefore look at the world and derive the תורה
### היינו שהשיגו ענייני הכחות העליונים
They understood how the מצוות affect the universe, thus they understood the "reasons" and everything they did was to have a positive on the universe
This was because it was all pre-מתן תורה.
