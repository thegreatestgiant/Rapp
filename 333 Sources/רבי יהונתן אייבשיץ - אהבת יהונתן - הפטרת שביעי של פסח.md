### רבי יהונתן אייבשיץ - אהבת יהונתן - הפטרת שביעי של פסח
When you write a book of Tanach, in order for it to be in Naviim, it must be written via Nevua (like שמואל), as opposed to Kesuvim, which are written with Ruach Hakodesh (like רות).
The difference is whether the author is aware what they are writing.
