### אשי ישראל - ספר אשי ישראל שבת ב׳ ע''א
How can we have [[עקירה]] w/o a [[הנחה]] and still call it an איסור דרבנן.
You can't leave the תחום (2k אמות out side city), so going 1k is a חצי שיעור? That doesn't make sense!
There is a difference between [[חצי שיעור]] and [[חצי מלאכה]]
[[חצי שיעור]]:
- When you do half of the action and it's not allowed
- You did a Melacha, but not enough to get מלכות
[[חצי מלאכה]]:
- You're just doing part of the thing that is אסור
- You didn't do anything wrong

The distinction is whatever the Rabbi's say
