### אמר רבי יוחנן
Why did he get to become king? Because he built up part of the land of Israel
### אחאב
Meaning of his name: אח to שמים, but an אב to עבודה זרה.
The lightest sins that אחאב did were as bad as the worst sins of ירבעם.
Why does the פסוק blame ירבעם then? Because ירבעם started the trend (תחילה לקלקלה).
### ויעש אחאב את האשרה
What did אחאב do to anger ה more than any king before him?
He wrote on the city gates of שומרון: "אחאב כפר באלקי ישראל" (Achav denies the God of Israel).
Because of this, he has no portion in the God of Israel.
