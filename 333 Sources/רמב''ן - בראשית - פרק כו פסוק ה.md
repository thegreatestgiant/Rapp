### ויש לשאול
How where they bringing קרבנות on a single stone מזבת, marrying sisters, and other עבירות?
A: אברהם found the תורה, but he was keeping it in the manner of someone who doesn't have to do the מצוות
They only kept the מצוות in ארץ ישראל.
