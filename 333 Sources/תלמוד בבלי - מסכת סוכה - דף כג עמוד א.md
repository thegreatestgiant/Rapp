### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת סוכה - דף כג עמוד א

There's a [['מח]] if you can make a סוכה on a boat. 2 תנאים where on a boat, built a סוכה and it blew away.
So if it could withstand a stron wind on land, then it is כשר on the sea
What if it's strong on land, but can't withstand the sea
The [['מח]] is wheather it's perm home and needs to be alive where it is, or if it's temporary and needs to be able to withstand most times
