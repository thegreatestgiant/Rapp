### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת מעוד קטן - דף ט''ז, עמוד ב
These are words of נבוא
This is just a follow up from last פרק and not the final words

Who rules over ה? The צדיקים who can overturn decrees

When דוד was in ישיבה he would sit uncomfortably (except when his רבי was there). Because דוד did this, ה said דוד could overturn his גזירות. He would be put before the אבות.
In learning he made himself small and weak like an insect
In war he was mighty as an oak tree. He bemoaned not hitting 1,000 with his arrows and only getting 800
