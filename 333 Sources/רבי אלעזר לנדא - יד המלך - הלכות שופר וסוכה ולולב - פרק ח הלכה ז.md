### רבי אלעזר לנדא - יד המלך - הלכות שופר וסוכה ולולב - פרק ח הלכה ז
If the stem falls off and there is a hole left there then it is פסול
When you take the Esrog it has to be complete with the Pitum it grew with
The act of the Pitum falling off, makes the Esrog פסול, so you can't put it back on, It would be the same as taking a Pitum from another Esrog, or turning 2 Esrogim into 1.
The [[רבי יעקב ריישר - שבות יעקב - חלק א - סימן לה]] is talking about when it is still attached by a thread, which is fine.
