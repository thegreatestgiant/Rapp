### המאירי - על מסכת סוטה - דף ב עמוד א
There is reward and punishment, nothing is random.
If you are worthy then you get a good wife, and if not then you get a bad wife
So [[בשערט]] is just default, but once you are are a בעל חוב then you get what you deserve
