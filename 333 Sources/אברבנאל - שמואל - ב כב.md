### אברבנאל - שמואל - ב כב
I think this song was written when he was a young lad. And whenever he overcame a trial, he would sing this song.
This is also something anyone can sing when they are at the end of their days.
Whenever you sin you should say this.
This is a general Tefillah, that was generalized by David from his more specific praises
