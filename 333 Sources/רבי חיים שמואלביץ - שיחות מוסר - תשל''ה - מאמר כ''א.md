### רבי חיים שמואלביץ - שיחות מוסר - תשל''ה - מאמר כ''א

So שלמה knows he has to kill שמעי, so he makes him promise to stay in ירושלים. But how could שלמה know that he would leave? Why did שמעי leave in the first place? Why do we care about swearing a נדר, the punishment for that is מלכות or a קרבן. Why are there warnings?
People want freedom, so if it's limited... What שלמה did was limit his freedom even more...
