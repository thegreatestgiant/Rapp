### משנה ברורה - משנה ברורה - סימן קכד סעיף ד
Don't learn during חהש''ץ even if you answer at the end of every ברכה
If you learn, you teach others you can do whatever you want like read a newspaper
The ש''ץ should have in mind that it might be a תפילת נדבה, if he might not have 9 answering him
You stand because if you listen it's as if you are יצא, and should stand. Defeniatly should stand if within 4 amos of the ש''ץ
