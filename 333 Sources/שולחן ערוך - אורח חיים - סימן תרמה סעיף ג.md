### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן תרמה סעיף ג
If there is a split in spine going down most of the way - פסול
Some say that you don't even have to crack the spine, but if the middle leaf is split until the spine then it is still פסול
Even though it's only a problem if it splits all the way down, try to buy something with no splits up top
