### בירור הלכה - שאלות על זיהוי התכלת - ההוכחות לזיהוי אינן מוחלטות
Reasons people don't wear / questions on identification:
1. Even if you're right, there's a lack of מסורה (no unbroken tradition identifying this snail).
2. Dyeing threads not in the color of the garment: on a white tallit, threads must be white; if this isn't real תכלת, acc. to [[רש''י]] you are מבטל עשה because it invalidates the ציצית.
3. Using counterfeit/fake dye: if it's not authentic תכלת, there is an issue of קלא אילן.
4. Issue of יוהרא (religious haughtiness/arrogance in adopting a non-standard stringency).
5. Midrashic sources state that the תכלת was נגנזה (hidden away).
6. Kabbalistic sources state that the spiritual light of תכלת does not apply in the absence of the בית המקדש, and will only return with the coming of משיח.
7. Descriptions of the חלזון in the גמרא (body resembles the sea, form like a fish, emerges once in 70 years, expensive) do not straightforwardly match the *Murex trunculus* (ארגמון קהה הקוצים).
