### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת סנהדרין - דף לח עמוד ב
There were 12 hours in the day when man was created.
1. The dirt was created
2. He was given form
3. The limbs were formed
4. He got a soul
5. He stood up
6. He named the animal
7. He married Chavah
8. They had 2 kids
9. They were commanded not to eat from the tree
10. They ate from the tree
11. They were judged
12. Tossed out
