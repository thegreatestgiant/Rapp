### רב עובדיה יוסף - שו''ת יחווה דעת - חלק ד סימן ב
Strictly speaking there is no obligation to wear a טלית קטן all day, but one should make every effort to wear it under clothes throughout the day.
One should educate children (חינוך) to wear a טלית קטן starting from age 6 (and some say from age 3, as "a man is like a tree of the field").
