### משך חכמה - בראשית - פרק טו פסוק יג
Ramban challenges Rambam: if someone fulfills Hashem's decree, shouldn't they be rewarded as "זריז ונשכר"?
Meshech Chochma answers: ה wasn't commanding the Egyptians to enslave them (not a ציווי), He was just predicting what would happen (דרך סיפור).
Also, the Egyptians were terrible ingrates (כפויי טובה)—Yosef saved them from starvation and made them wealthy, yet they turned around and oppressed his family.
