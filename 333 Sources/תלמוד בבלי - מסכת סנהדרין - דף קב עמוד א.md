### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת סנהדרין - דף קב עמוד א

### melachim 1.11
- What does it mean a new cloak? Just like a new cloak has no flaw (*אין בה שום דופי*), so too the תורה of ירבעם was completely without flaw.
- Alternatively, they innovated Torah concepts that no ear had ever heard before.

### melachim 1.12
- Nothing good ever happens in שכם: it is a place designated for calamity (*מקום מזומן לפורענות*).
  - In שכם, Dinah was afflicted.
  - In שכם, Yosef was sold by his brothers.
  - In שכם, the kingdom of the House of David was divided.
