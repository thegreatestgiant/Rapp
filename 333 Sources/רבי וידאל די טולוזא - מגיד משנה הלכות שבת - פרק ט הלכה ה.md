### רבי וידאל די טולוזא - מגיד משנה הלכות שבת - פרק ט הלכה ה
- **Gemara vs. Rambam**: The Gemara (Shabbos 3b–4a) states they permitted scraping the bread before he incurs *איסור סקילה* (implying במזיד), yet the Rambam writes *"one who forgot"* (שכח, meaning בשוגג).
- **Resolution**: The sugya concludes that this permission applies not just to במזיד, but **even בשוגג** (to save him from a חטאת).
- **Kal Vachomer (וכל שכן)**: Rambam chose to state *שוגג* because that is the bigger novelty; and **all the more so** (וכל שכן) it is permitted for *במזיד* to save him from actual סקילה.
