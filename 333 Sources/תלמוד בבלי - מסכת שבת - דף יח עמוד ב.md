### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת שבת - דף יח עמוד ב

Where does it say a women shouldn't take a root vegetable and put it up before Shabbos because it takes a long time to cook

You can't even eat it after Shabbos until you wait the amount of time it would take to cook
A baker shouldn't put a barrel of water next to fire, because it takes a long time
This is because you might stoke the coals
For cloth and spices it's ok because you don't want it to be to hot
**What about wool? No, you might stir the pot**
