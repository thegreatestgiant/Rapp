### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן לד
You put them into the בתים in what aredoer?
[[רש''י]] - Everything in order
[[ר''ת]] - Flip the last 2
Those who fear ה wear both. You have [[רש''י]] for most of davening until אשריish. But only חסידים really do this.
You can't put them into the same time because 1 pair isn't [[תפילין]]
