### הרב צבי שכטר - נפש הרב - ד׳ קכ''ג וקכ''ה
There is a תפילה of and a תפילה by the ציבור. You should stand with your feet together during חהש''ץ because it's on the behalf of the entire ציבור
This is 1 תפילה said by 1 person on behalf of everybody. So if someone can't make it to shul, the ש''ץ can have you in mind since it's comparable to a קרבן תמיד
