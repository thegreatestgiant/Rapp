### רמב''ם - הלכות תלמוד תורה - פרק ד הלכה ו
When a Rabbi walks in, first let him sit down
Not too many people at a time
Don't ask him something different, ask about what you are learning know
The Rabbi can trick them to see if they are sharp 
Rebbi can ask a Student on any topic
