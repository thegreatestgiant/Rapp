### רמב''ם - הלכות יסודי התורה - פרק ז הלכה ז
- **Two Categories of נבואה:**
  1. *Personal נבואה (נבואתו לעצמו בלבד):* Given purely to expand the prophet's own mind and knowledge of sublime divine mysteries.
  2. *Public Mission (שליחות):* Sent to a nation, city, or kingdom to guide them, instruct them in what to do, or warn them to turn back from their evil deeds (the archetype of the איש אלקים sent from יהודה to rebuke ירבעם at the altar in בית אל).
- **Role of the אות ומופת:**
  - A prophet sent on a public mission is given an אות ומופת (a sign/wonder) so the people recognize that Hashem genuinely sent him (explaining why the איש אלקים immediately backed up his words with the miraculous splitting of the מזבח and pouring out of its ashes).
- **Prerequisites for Believing a נביא:**
  - We do *not* believe anyone simply because they perform an אות ומופת. 
  - The baseline requirement is that he was already established from the outset as worthy of נבואה through his wisdom, piety, and ascetic separation (קדושה ופרישות), rising above his peers. Only once those credentials exist does his אות obligate us to obey him (*"אליו תשמעון"*).
