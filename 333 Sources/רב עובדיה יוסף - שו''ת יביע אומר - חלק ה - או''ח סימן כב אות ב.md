### אות ב
You're not חייב in קידוש because you aren't allowed to eat without a מקום סעודה. So if you are between שחרית ומוסף, then you can eat something
Either you always eat before קידוש, or you can eat before מוסף because there is no חיוב given that you haven't davened yet.
As a ספרדי you can eat before מוסף
