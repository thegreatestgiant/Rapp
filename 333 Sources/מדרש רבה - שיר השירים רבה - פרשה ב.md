### מדרש רבה - שיר השירים רבה - פרשה ב
- **Identity of the "נביא אחד זקן" (1 Kings 13:11):**
  - **ר' מאיר:** It was אמציה כהן בית אל (the priest of the calves).
  - **ר' יוסי:** Dismisses this; it was **יונתן בן גרשם בן משה** (Moses's own grandson from שופטים י"ח; the "נו"ן תלויה" in "מנשה" hints that his conduct reflected מנשה (the evil king) rather than his grandfather משה).
- **How Did an Idolatrous Priest Live for Hundreds of Years?**
  - He secretly undermined the עבודה זרה ("היתה עינו צרה בע"ז"). He would dissuade worshippers by pointing out how absurd it is for an elderly person (50–80 years old) to bow down to an idol manufactured just a few years ago.
  - When pressed on why he worked there, he explained he was just taking a salary while sabotaging it from within.
  - **The Distorted Family Tradition:** He claimed: *"Sell yourself to ע"ז rather than need help from people."* דוד המלך corrected him: it means do *alien/degrading work* (עבודה שזרה ממך), not literal idol worship! David appointed him royal treasurer (*קומסין תסברין*), but once David died, he relapsed back to his old ways.
- **The Lie to the איש אלקים & The קל וחומר:**
  - He tricked the איש אלקים by falsely claiming an angel told him to feed him bread (*"כחש לו - האכילהו לחם כוזבים"*).
  - Even though he lied and deceived him, Hashem still granted him a genuine נבואה while sitting at the table!
  - **The Power of Hospitality (קל וחומר):** If someone who lied and fed someone deceitful bread still merited רוח הקודש simply because he provided food, how much more so will righteous people who warmly host Torah scholars (מאכל, משתה, ומטה) be rewarded by הקב"ה!
