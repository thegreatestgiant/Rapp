### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת יומא - דף ט עמוד ב
If all of כלל ישראל came back from בבל with עזרא like a solid wall (חומה), then בית שני would've been like silver where decay never takes hold (the שכינה would've rested fully and it wouldn't have been destroyed).
Instead only a trickle came back ("like doors" - דלתות), so they were like cedar wood which rots away.
Takeaway: בית שני was inherently deficient from day one. No ארון, no שכינה, and doomed to be destroyed because most Jews stayed in בבל.
