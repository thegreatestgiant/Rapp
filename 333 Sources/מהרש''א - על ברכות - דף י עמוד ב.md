### מהרש''א - על ברכות - דף י עמוד ב - melachim 1.14
the Gemara's reading of *gavecha* (גוך - your back) as *ge'echa* (גאיך - your haughtiness/pride) is not just a homiletic play on words for someone who eats before davening; it is the literal reason the Navi Achiya said this to Yarov'am in Melachim I 14:9. Yarov'am was offered Gan Eden directly by Hashem, but his pride and ego refused to let him walk second behind David, so he cast Hashem aside over personal honor.
