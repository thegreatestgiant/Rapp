### מגן אברהם - אורח חיים - סימן רנד ס''ק כב
- **Kushya (*Kikayon De-Yonah*)**: Why do we waive the rabbinic prohibition (שבות) of scraping bread to prevent *כרת / סקילה*? In other areas (like not sprinkling purification water on Erev Pesach, or not bringing a circumcision scalpel), Chazal *upheld* their decrees (*העמידו דבריהם*) even when it caused someone to incur *כרת*!
- **Answer**: This is not a contradiction. As the Gemara shows in *Pesachim 92a*, there are many instances where Chazal did **not** uphold their decrees in the face of *כרת*. Those other cases are exceptions, not an absolute rule.
