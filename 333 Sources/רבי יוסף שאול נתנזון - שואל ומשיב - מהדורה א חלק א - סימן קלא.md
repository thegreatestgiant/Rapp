### רבי יוסף שאול נתנזון - שואל ומשיב - מהדורה א חלק א - סימן קלא
Someone's Pitum split in half, and the ball part fell off. I told him to stick it back on with a pin, in such a way you can't see it so that way it won't be [[בעל תוסף]]
The little bally (שושנתה) on top of the Pitum isn't required for it to be כשר
