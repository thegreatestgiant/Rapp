### רמב''ן - מסכת שבת - דף יח עמוד ב

Stirring is אסור according to רש''י because it is מבשל
I disagree. Before we only cared about stirring the coals, not stirring the cholent.
People are more likely to stir the pot, and not the coals. So the problem with the wool, would be צובע, to make it equally dyed.
What about the [[תלמוד בבלי - מסכת ביצה - דף לד עמוד א]]? There is a difference between dying and cooking, dying requires constant stirring, cooking only needs once in the beginning. So the first isn't allowed but the rest?
Final verdict: You are פטור אבל אסור

Maybe it only applies to things that are cooked
