### זוג ראשון
- **זוג ראשון** (First match) – Based on *mazal* (predetermined fate / decreed before birth).
- **זוג שני** (Second match) – Based on one's deeds/merits, and it is difficult to pair them because she was not originally his destined partner (*בת זוגו*).
