### רמב''ם - הלכות נדרים - פרק ג הלכה ט
If someone swears they will fast on י''ט or שבת, they have to fast.
If you swear you'll fast every Monday... and י''ט falls out on Monday, you have to fast, if it's חנוכה ופורים then you don't fast because we need to strengthen the דרבנן
