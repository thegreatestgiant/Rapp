### רש''ש - מסכת שבת - דף עג עמוד א
What is assumed, is that you are not עובר the איסור, until the bread rises.
So if you put seed and put it on the ground, at what point are you עובר the איסור? When it's on the ground or when it takes root. Until it takes root, it's מותר
What if you put bread in the oven 5 minutes before שבת ends, can you stop the אפיה from שבת? If it works by planting it works here.
