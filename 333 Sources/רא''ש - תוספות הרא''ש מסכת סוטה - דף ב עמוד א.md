### רא''ש - תוספות הרא''ש מסכת סוטה - דף ב עמוד א
- **הא בזוג ראשון הא בזוג שני**: It is only considered a "second match" (governed by deeds and difficult to pair) if it is a 2nd marriage for **both** of them. If it is a 1st marriage for either one, that person's match was already decreed before their formation.
