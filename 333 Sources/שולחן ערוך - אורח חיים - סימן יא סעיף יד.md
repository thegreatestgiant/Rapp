### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן יא סעיף יד
Double knot, wrap,  double knot, wrap.
Should end up with 4 wraps and 5 ties.
There is no specific rule of how many wraps, it should just be 4 thumb-widths for the knotted section and twice as much for the hanging strings

The tradition we have it -> 7,9,11,13. This adds up to 40
Some people add little knots at the end
