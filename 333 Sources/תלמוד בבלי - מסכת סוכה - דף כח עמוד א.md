### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת סוכה - דף כח עמוד א
You should have your head and most of your body and your table inside of you סוכה.
They went to a Rabbi, and he was sitting with his table outside the סוכה.
The elders of ב''ה and ב''ש said that you would never be קיים the מצוה in your life
