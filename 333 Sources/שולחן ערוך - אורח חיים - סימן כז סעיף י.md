### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן כז סעיף י
Behind the head above the neck, equivalent to between the eyes. It shouldn't be moving. The knot should look like a ד
Don't cover your של ראש and if your רבי comes by then you should take it off
#### [[רמ''א]]
You can have your [[תפילין]] be uncovered in front of your רבי
