### רמב''ם - הלכות עבודה זרה - פרק י הלכה ו
When Jews have the upper hand (שיד ישראל תקיפה), we are forbidden to allow idolaters to dwell among us in Eretz Yisrael.
Even temporary residence (ישיבת עראי) or passing through for business (עובר בסחורה) is forbidden until he accepts the 7 מצוות בני נח (making him a [[גר תושב]]).
We only accept a [[גר תושב]] when the יובל is in effect; when יובל is not in effect, only a [[גר צדק]] is accepted.
#### [[ראב''ד|ראב"ד]]
- On passing through: We never found or heard of such a thing. The verse refers to the 7 nations, and it only specifies dwelling (ישיבה), not passing through (העברה).
- On יובל: Disagrees regarding dwelling in Eretz Yisrael (holds that even when יובל is not in effect, a non-Jew who accepts the 7 mitzvot may dwell in Eretz Yisrael).
