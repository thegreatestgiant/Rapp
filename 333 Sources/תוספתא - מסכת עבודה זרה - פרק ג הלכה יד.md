### תוספתא - מסכת עבודה זרה - פרק ג הלכה יד
When does the איסור of מתנת חנם (giving a free gift to a non-Jew) apply?
Only to a stranger you don't know or someone passing through.
If he is a friend or neighbor, it's allowed because it's not a free gift—it's like selling it to him (מוכרו לו), since you get friendship or favors in return.
