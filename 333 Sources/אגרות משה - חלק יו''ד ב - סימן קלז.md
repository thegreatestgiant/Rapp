### אגרות משה - חלק יו''ד ב - סימן קלז
At night, if you took your ציצית off, (for a shower...): You are able to put them back on, even thought the [[מגן אברהם]] says you can't
There might even be a מצווה, but defeniatley no ברכה
There is no אסור to put them on at night
