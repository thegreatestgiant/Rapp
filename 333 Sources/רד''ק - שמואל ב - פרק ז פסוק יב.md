### אשר יצא ממעיך
Your son that will be born of you will be king - GOD. At that time, both אדוניה and אבשלום were alive, so they weren't in the running.
This is why his name was שלמה ידידיה
Also the name שלמה symbolized that he would brting a period of peace to the Jewish people.
