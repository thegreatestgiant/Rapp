### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן יב סעיף א
If you string ripped and you can tie a bow, you're good.
If one of them goes down to nothing you're out (פסול). But it's not 1/8, but 1/4.
If 2/8 go down, then they might be the same string. You could be careful with keeping the same 4 on each side
