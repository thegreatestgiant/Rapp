### תנ''ך - זכריה - פרק ד
זכריה has a vision of a pure gold מנורה with two olive trees on each side feeding oil straight into the bowls automatically.
He asks the מלאך: What does this mean?
The מלאך says this is the message to זרבבל:
"לא בחיל ולא בכח כי אם ברוחי" - Not by physical strength or an army, but by My spirit.
And זרבבל will bring out the headstone (אבן הראשה) of the ב''ה with shouts of "חן חן לה".
The two olive trees are the "שני בני היצהר" (the two anointed ones: זרבבל representing מלכות and יהושע כהן גדול representing כהונה).
