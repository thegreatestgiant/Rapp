### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת ברכות - דף י עמוד ב - melachim 1.14
Rabbi Yitzchak said that Rabbi Yochanan said that Rabbi Yosei bar Rabbi Chanina said in the name of Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov:
Anyone who eats and drinks and only afterward prays, regarding him the verse states: *"And You have cast Me behind your back [gavecha]"* (Melachim I 14:9) — do not read it as *gavecha* [your back], but rather as *ge'echa* [your pride/haughtiness]. The Holy One, Blessed be He, said: Only after this person acted haughtily did he accept upon himself the Kingdom of Heaven.
