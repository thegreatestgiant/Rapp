### הרב שלמה יוסף זוין - ספר מועדים בהלכה - דף נ''ג
1905 - Someone named Akiva Yosef Shelzinger. He was of the opinion that nowadays you have the right to and should blow שופר on Rosh Hashanah on Shabbos in Yerushalayim
- According to the ramabm, the cityu of ירושלים never had the גזירע
- From the year 400-1000 they kept 1 day of ראש השנה. The only reason we have 2 days of ר''ה in א''י is because of the [[רב יוסף אלבו]], so either follow him and keep 2 days and blow on שבת or only keep 1 day, and blow on שבת anyways.
Lots of people said yes and lots of people said no way. He did in fact blow on שבת.
