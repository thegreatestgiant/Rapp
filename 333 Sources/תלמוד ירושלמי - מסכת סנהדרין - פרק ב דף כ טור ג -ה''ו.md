### תלמוד ירושלמי - מסכת סנהדרין - פרק ב דף כ טור ג -ה''ו
He would pave the roads with silver and no one would take the pavement
He had solver bricks that were 10x8 amos
Even the weight stones where made of gold
When ה saw him wasting money he told him to get off his throne. So ה replaced שלמה with a doppelganger and he wandered the streets as a begger
