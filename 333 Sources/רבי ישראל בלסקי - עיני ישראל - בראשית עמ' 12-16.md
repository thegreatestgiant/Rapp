### רבי ישראל בלסקי - עיני ישראל - בראשית עמ' 12-16
The big bang says everything started with nothing (תוהו) and it combined with בוהו and became ever expanding, this is in accordance with the [[רמב''ן]], who says things came with a really small particle, and is ever expanding
The destruction of old worlds now fuels our own world.

The original idea of there being previous iterations of this world that have been destroyed and built upon is true. And each consecutive world is better and built upon the last.
Combined all of the worlds might be billions of years, but this world is 5787 years old.
