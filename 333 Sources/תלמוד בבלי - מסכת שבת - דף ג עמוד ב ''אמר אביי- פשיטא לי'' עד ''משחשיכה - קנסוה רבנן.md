### תלמוד בבלי - מסכת שבת - דף ג עמוד ב ''אמר אביי- פשיטא לי'' עד ''משחשיכה - קנסוה רבנן
A [[כרמלית]] is not [[רשות הרבים]] or like a [[רשות היחיד]]. So what is it?
One says once you stick your hand out you can't bring it back in.
It's either like a [[כרמלית]] or not. No, e/o agrees it's like a [[כרמלית]]. The question is if it's more than 10 טפח above the ground.
The question is at what part of the day it is. If it's later in the day then you get a כנס, or rather if your hand was there before nightime (שבת)

It's either by time or above the ground.
