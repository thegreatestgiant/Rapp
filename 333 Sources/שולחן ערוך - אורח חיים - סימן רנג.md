### שולחן ערוך - אורח חיים - סימן רנג

Make sure not to pour water into a boiling pot. Because this is a method of [[מגיס| Stirring]].
Scooping out food from still uncooked pot on the fire is also [[מגיס|Stirring]]
You can scoop once it's edible
