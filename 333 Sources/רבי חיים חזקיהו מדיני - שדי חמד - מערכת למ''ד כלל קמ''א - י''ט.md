### רבי חיים חזקיהו מדיני - שדי חמד - מערכת למ''ד כלל קמ''א - י''ט
If you stuck it back in with a pin, what's the deal? Everyone disagrees and says it's not ok.
The truth is, every time you have 2 that need to be 1, you can stick it back together. This will make it 1, but not complete
