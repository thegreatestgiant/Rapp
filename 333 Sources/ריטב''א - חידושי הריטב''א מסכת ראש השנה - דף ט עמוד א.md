### ריטב''א - חידושי הריטב''א מסכת ראש השנה - דף ט עמוד א
It says in pesahcim that there was a guy who would fast every day of the year besides for ערב יום כיפור...
We fast on י''כ to be like angels, so we don't eat. but they also don't get hungry, so we eat beforehand to be less hungry and be more angel like
