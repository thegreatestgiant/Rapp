### רבי יעקב בן אשר - טור אורח חיים - סימן קיד
From the [[מהר''ם מרוטנבורק]]:  It isn't only 30 days, but it's about saying it 90 times (3 times a day for 30 days)
If you say it 90 times, then you will be patterned to say it so you won't have to repeat if you forget

How do we know its about how many times, and not about the 30 days?
We learn it from [[מועד]], if an ox gore's 3 times in a row, we don't say that he just has a bad day, he is still a [[מועד]].
