### רבי צדקיה בן אברהם הרופא - שבלי הלקט סדר יום הכיפורים - סימן שז
Some say that eating before י''כ makes it easier, and that's why we are commanded to do so
Others say that if you eat a lot, your stomach expands and makes you hungrier the next day, this will increase the hunger the next day, which makes it harder and increases the suffering
Others say the צדוקין would fast on 9th, so we specifically eat on the 9th
