### קצות החושן - חושן משפט - סימן קעא ס''ק א
You are not allowed to give your עבד degrading work - [[עבודת פרח]]
You can give [[עבודת פרח]] to a half-free slave
The [[רשב''א]] says that because he's half-free then you can just free him. It means you can't treat him like a slave, and you can give him [[עבודת פרח]] so he's already not being treated like a slave
