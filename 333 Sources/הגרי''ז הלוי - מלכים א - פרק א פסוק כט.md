### הגרי''ז הלוי - מלכים א - פרק א פסוק כט
Why are we saying long live דוד, it should be long live שלמה.
The רמב''ם says in order to be משיח you must be a decendand of שלמה as well. The completion of דוד monarchy, is the establishing of שלמה.
