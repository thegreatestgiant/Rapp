### ג׳ קדושות
This is a misprint, there is really 4, because ברכו counts as a קדושות
We don't count קדיש יתום.
Some say to wait for the קדיש after עלינו.
Don't wrap up your [[תפילין]] while listening to קדיש and יהי שמא רבה is very important, and you shouldn't be doing anything else then
