### יצחק אלחנן ספקטור - שו''ת באר יצחק חלק אבן העזר - סימן א
Someone gets married, and has a son in שבט, and dies before פורים, and the mother was nursing. But the mother became sick so they had to hire a wet nurse.
Someone says they will marry and support her, but now he can't marry her because she is 'nursing'. He said he won't wait that long, and she has to get lost if she won't marry her. 
This will cause her to turn to the worlds oldest profession to sustain herself
A local Rabbi said they should get married.
In a normal situation we appoint an apputrupus (guardian), who can sell the slaves on behalf of the child. But isn't that a sin? You can still do it.
But according to the [[רשב''א]] the reason it was allowed was because he was half-freed. I don't understand him. So there is an issue of [[חצי שיעור]]
