### פטור אבל אסור
There's no issue of שבת, but there was an issue of [[לפני עור]]. If that person was a מומר then it would still be an issue
You are not allowed to lend tools (hammer) on שבת if you suspect he will be doing מלכה with it. Unless there is a reasonable thought process that he's going to do something permitted with it like cracking walnuts
When it comes to non-Jews, if he can do it by himself then it's fine
