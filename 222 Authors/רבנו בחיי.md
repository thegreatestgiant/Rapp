# רבנו בחיי

## Background Info
Rabbeinu Bahya ben Asher ibn Halawa (c. 1255–1340), one of the preeminent early Spanish Rishonim and biblical commentators. A disciple of the Rashba (Rabbi Shlomo ben Aderet). His celebrated Torah commentary integrates the four interpretive methods of Pardes (Peshat, Remez, Derash, and Sod); he also authored the ethical work *Kad HaKemach*.
