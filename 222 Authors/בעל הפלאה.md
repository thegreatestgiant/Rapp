# בעל הפלאה

## Background Info
Rabbi Pinchas HaLevi Horowitz (ה'תצ"א–ה'תקס"ה, 1731–1805), known as the **Ba'al HaHafla'ah** (בעל הפלאה). One of the preeminent sages of Ashkenaz, disciple of the Maggid of Mezeritch (המגיד ממעזריטש), and Chief Rabbi of Frankfurt am Main (פרנקפורט דמיין) for over thirty years. Named after his monumental work **Sefer HaHafla'ah** (**ספר הפלאה**), which includes **Sefer HaMikneh** (**ספר המקנה** on Kiddushin), **Sefer Ketubah** (**ספר כתובה** on Ketubot), and his Torah commentary **Panim Yafot** (**פנים יפות**).
