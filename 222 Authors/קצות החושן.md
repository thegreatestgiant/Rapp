# קצות החושן

## Background Info
Rabbi Aryeh Leib Heller (רבי אריה לייב הלר, c. 1745–1812 / ה'תק"ה–ה'תקע"ג), celebrated as **Ba'al Ha-Ktzot** (**בעל קצות החושן**), Rav of Stryi. His masterwork *Ktzot HaChoshen* (**קצות החושן**) on the *Shulchan Aruch* (*Choshen Mishpat*) is one of the most foundational and transformative analytic texts in all of Yeshiva learning, alongside his conceptual legal treatise *Shev Shema'tata* (**שב שמעתתא**) and his work on *Even HaEzer*, *Avnei Milu'im* (**אבני מלואים**).
