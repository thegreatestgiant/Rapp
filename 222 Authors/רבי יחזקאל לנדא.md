# רבי יחזקאל לנדא

## Background Info
Rabbi Yechezkel Landau (רבי יחזקאל סג"ל לנדא, 1713–1793 / ה'תע"ד–ה'תקנ"ג), one of the supreme halachic authorities of the Acharonim, Chief Rabbi and Rosh Yeshiva of Prague (פראג) and Bohemia. Known as the **Noda BiYehudah** after his monumental responsa collection **Noda BiYehudah** (**שו"ת נודע ביהודה**, Mahadura Kama and Tinyana), and author of the classic Talmudic commentary **Tziyun LeNefesh Chaya** (**צל"ח / Tzelach**).
