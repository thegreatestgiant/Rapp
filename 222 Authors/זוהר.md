# זוהר

## Background Info
Canonical mystical work of the Jewish esoteric tradition, foundational to Kabbalah, traditionally attributed to Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai (Rashbi) and his circle. Includes major sections such as the main commentary on the Torah, Ra'aya Meheimna, Tikkunei HaZohar, and Midrash HaNe'elam.
