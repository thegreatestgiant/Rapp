# רבי בנימין יהושע זילבר

## Background Info
- **Lifespan**: 1916–2008 (ה'תרע"ו–כ"ד באלול ה'תשס"ח)
- **Background & Training**: Born in Mazheik (Mažeikiai), Lithuania. In his youth, he studied in the Telz Yeshiva and the Navardok (Novardok) yeshiva system, deeply imbibing Navardok's intense mussar ethos and total absorption in Torah. He made aliyah to Mandatory Palestine in 1933 (תרצ"ג), residing in Magdiel and later establishing himself in Bnei Brak (בני ברק).
- **Status & Independence**: For decades he lived in ascetic devotion and study (*perishut*), initially avoiding formal communal rabbinic appointments. His encyclopedic mastery and fearlessness in halakhic adjudication earned him immense respect across Lithuanian, Hasidic, and Sephardic communities alike. In his later years, he served as an influential member of the Moetzes Gedolei HaTorah (מועצת גדולי התורה) of Agudath Israel.
- **Works**: Best known for his monumental 14-volume responsa collection **Az Nidberu** (**שו"ת אז נדברו**), addressing complex modern halakhic questions across all four sections of Shulchan Aruch with direct reliance on primary Talmudic and Rishonic sources. He also authored *Mishnas Binyamin* (commentary on *Sefer Charedim* / ספר חרדים), *Shevitat HaShabbat* (*שביתת השבת* on the 39 Melachot), *Torat HaHistaklut*, and annotations to Rabbeinu Yonah's *Sha'arei Teshuvah*.
