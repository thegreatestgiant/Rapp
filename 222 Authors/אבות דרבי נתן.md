# אבות דרבי נתן

## Background Info
A minor tractate (*masechet ketana*) from the Tannaitic period that expands upon and expounds *Pirkei Avot* (מסכת אבות), attributed to the Tanna Rabbi Natan HaBavli (רבי נתן הבבלי).
