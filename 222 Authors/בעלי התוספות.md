# בעלי התוספות

## Background Info
The **Ba'alei HaTosafot** (בעלי התוספות) were medieval French and German scholars during the 12th and 13th centuries who continued the tradition of Rashi (רש''י)—many being his grandsons and disciples, such as Rabbeinu Tam (רבנו תם), the Ri HaZaken (ר''י הזקן), and Rashbam (רשב''ם). Their commentaries on the Talmud are known as the *Tosafot*. On the Torah, the famous compilation **Da'at Zekenim** (**דעת זקנים מבעלי התוספות**) anthologizes their insights and rulings across the Chumash.
