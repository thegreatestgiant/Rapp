# אבודרהם

## Background Info
Rabbi David ben Yosef Abudarham (רבי דוד אבודרהם, 14th century / המאה ה-14), renowned Rishon in Seville, Spain. Disciple of the Ba'al HaTurim (Rabbi Yaakov ben Asher). Famous author of *Sefer Abudarham* (*ספר אבודרהם* / *חיבור פירוש הברכות והתפילות*), the classic foundational commentary on the Siddur, liturgy, laws of prayer, and Jewish calendar.
