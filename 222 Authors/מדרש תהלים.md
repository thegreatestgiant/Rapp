# מדרש תהלים

## Background Info
Midrash Tehillim (מדרש תהלים), also historically known as **Shocher Tov** (שוחר טוב) from its opening verse (Proverbs 11:27), is an aggadic midrash on the Book of Psalms compiled from tannaitic, amoraic, and later rabbinic teachings. The standard critical edition was prepared and annotated by Solomon Buber (Vilna, 1891).
