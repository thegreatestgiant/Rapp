# רבי שמואל הלוי וואזנר

## Background Info
Rabbi Shmuel HaLevi Wosner (רבי שמואל הלוי וואזנר, 1913–2015 / תרע"ג–תשע"ה), prominent 20th and 21st-century posek, Rav and Rosh Yeshiva of Yeshivat Chachmei Lublin in Bnei Brak, and one of the preeminent halachic authorities of his era. Author of the renowned 11-volume responsa series **Shevet HaLevi** (**שו"ת שבט הלוי**).
