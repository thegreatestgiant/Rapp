# רבי יוסף ענגיל

**Rav Yosef Engel** (1858–1919), also spelled **יוסף אנגל** / **יוסף ענגיל**. Av Beit Din of Kraków, Poland. Prolific author of Torah scholarship.

## Major Works

- **גליוני הש"ס** (*Gilyonei HaShas*) — glosses and chiddushim on Shas Bavli & Yerushalmi (Zeraim, Moed, Nashim, Nezikin)
- **אתוון דאורייתא** — on the 39 melachot of Shabbat
- **שבעים פנים לתורה** (*Shiv'im Panim LaTorah*) / **לקח טוב**
- **אסוון דאורייתא** (*Esvan D'Oraysa*)
- **בית האוצר** (*Beis HaOtzar*) — Talmudic encyclopedia
- **גבורות שמונים** — 80 answers on a single sugya

## Background

Born in Tarnów (Galicia), 1 Tevet 5619 (1858). Studied under Rav Shimon Sofer (son of Chatam Sofer) and Rav Meir Arik. Served as Rav in Dzików, then Av Beit Din in Kraków from 1900 until his petira 1 Cheshvan 5680 (1919). Known for his encyclopedic knowledge and novel analytical approach.
