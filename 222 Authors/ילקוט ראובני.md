# ילקוט ראובני

## Background Info
**Yalkut Reuveni** (**ילקוט ראובני**) is a major 17th-century anthological commentary on the Torah compiled by **Rabbi Reuven ben Hoshke (Höschke) Sofer Kohen** of Prague (died 1673). The work is celebrated for collecting and organizing rare Kabbalistic, Midrashic, and mystical traditions according to the order of the Torah portions and verses, synthesizing teachings from the *Zohar*, early Kabbalists, Midrashim, and manuscripts.
