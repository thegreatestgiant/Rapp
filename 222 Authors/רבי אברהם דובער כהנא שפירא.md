# רבי אברהם דובער כהנא שפירא

## Background Info
Rabbi Avraham DovBer Kahana Shapiro (1870–1943), the last Chief Rabbi of Kovno (Kaunas), Lithuania, and president of the Agudas HaRabbanim of Lithuania. Renowned as one of the foremost Lithuanian halakhic decisors of the 20th century, he is known as the *Dvar Avraham* after his celebrated three-volume responsa collection.
