# הרב שלמה אבינר

## Background Info
Rabbi Shlomo Chaim HaKohen Aviner (הרב שלמה חיים הכהן אבינר, born 1943 / ה'תש"ג), leading Religious Zionist rosh yeshiva, president of Yeshivat Ateret Yerushalayim (formerly עטרת כהנים), former rabbi of Beit El (בית אל), and prolific author of dozens of works in Halacha, Jewish thought, responsa, and Torah articles.
