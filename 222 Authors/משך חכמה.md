# משך חכמה

## Background Info
Rabbi Meir Simcha of Dvinsk (רבי מאיר שמחה הכהן מדווינסק, 1843–1926 / תר"ג–תרפ"ו), preeminent leader, posek, and thinker of Eastern European Jewry. Celebrated for his two masterworks: **Ohr Somayach** (**אור שמח**) on the Rambam's *Mishneh Torah*, and his profound, philosophically oriented Torah commentary **Meshech Chochmah** (**משך חכמה**).
