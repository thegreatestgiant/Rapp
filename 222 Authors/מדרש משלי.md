# מדרש משלי

## Background Info
An aggadic midrash on the Book of Proverbs (ספר משלי), dating to the Geonic or late Amoraic period. A renowned critical edition was published by scholar Salomon (Shlomo) Buber (שלמה בובר, Vilna 1893 / וילנא תרנ"ג).
