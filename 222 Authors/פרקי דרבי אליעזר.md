# פרקי דרבי אליעזר

## Background Info
A Tannaitic / Midrashic aggadic work traditionally attributed to the Tanna Rabbi Eliezer ben Hyrcanus (רבי אליעזר בן הורקנוס). The work expounds biblical narrative from Creation through the era of the Tanakh, characterized by vivid narrative aggadah, liturgical motifs, and traditions of calendar and astronomical calculation.
