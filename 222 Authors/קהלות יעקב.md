# קהלות יעקב

## Background Info
Rabbi Yaakov Yisrael Kanievsky (רבי יעקב ישראל קניבסקי, 1899–1985 / ה'תרנ"ט–ה'תשמ"ה), universally known as **the Steipler Gaon** (**הסטייפלר**) or after his monumental multi-volume series **Kehillot Yaakov** (**קהלות יעקב**) spanning all tractates of the Talmud. Revered gadol, rosh yeshiva of Novardok in Pinsk and later in Bnei Brak, spiritual guide to generations of yeshiva scholars, and father of Rabbi Chaim Kanievsky.
