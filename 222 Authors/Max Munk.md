# Max Munk

## Background Info
Educator, researcher, and talmid chacham. Published Torah-scientific articles on geometric and mathematical issues in Tanach and the Talmud, including the noted article "שלוש בעיות הנדסיות בתנ''ך ובתלמוד" ("Three Geometric Problems in the Bible and Talmud"), which addresses calculations of the circumference of the Sea of Solomon (ים של שלמה, I Kings 7:23) and approximations of Pi ($\pi$).
