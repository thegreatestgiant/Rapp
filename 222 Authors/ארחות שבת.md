# ארחות שבת

## Background Info
A comprehensive and fundamental halachic work on the laws of Shabbat (הלכות שבת), co-authored by Rabbi Shalom Yosef Gelber (הרב שלום יוסף גלבר) and Rabbi Yitzchak Mordechai Rubin (הרב יצחק מרדכי רובין, Rav of Kehillat Bnei Torah in Har Nof / הר נוף). The work has become one of the most widely studied and authoritative contemporary reference sets on hilchot Shabbat.
