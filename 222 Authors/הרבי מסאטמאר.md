# הרבי מסאטמאר

## Background Info
Rabbi Yoel Teitelbaum (תרמ"ז–תשל"ט / 1887–1979), known as the **Satmar Rebbe** (**הרבי מסאטמאר**) or the **VaYoel Moshe** (ויואל משה). Founder and leader of the Satmar Hasidic movement in the United States and one of the most prominent Haredi leaders of the 20th century. Served as Rav in Carei (קראלי) and Satu Mare (סאטמאר) in Europe, survived the Holocaust aboard the Kasztner train, and rebuilt Satmar in Williamsburg, New York. Known for his staunch ideological opposition to Zionism and modernity, his halachic rulings, and major works including *VaYoel Moshe* (*ויואל משה*), *Al HaGeulah VeAl HaTemurah* (*על הגאולה ועל התמורה*), and *Divrei Yoel* (*דברי יואל* on Chumash, festivals, and responsa). On creation and the age of the universe, addressed the topic in his treatises on "destroyed worlds" (*olamot she-charvu* / עולמות שחרבו).
