# רב יעקב בצלאל זולטי

## Background Info
- **Lifespan**: 1920–1982 (תר"פ–תשמ"ג)
- **Role**: Chief Ashkenazi Rabbi of Jerusalem (רבה הראשי של ירושלים), Dayan on the Supreme Rabbinical Court (בית הדין הרבני הגדול), and Rosh Yeshiva of Yeshivas Semichas Chachamim.
- **Works**: Known for his masterwork *Mishnas Yaavetz* (*משנת יעב"ץ*) on Shas, Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch, featuring profound lamdanus and analytical clarity in brisk style.
