# רבי יהודה דוד אייזנשטיין

## Background Info
He has a display on the 4th floor of the library
He wrote Encyclopedias in the early 19th century

Rabbi Judah David Eisenstein (1854–1935 / תרי"ה–תרצ"ה), renowned American-Jewish anthologist, author, and encyclopedist. Author and editor of the famous **Otzar Midrashim** (**אוצר מדרשים**), an exhaustive two-volume compendium of two hundred smaller Midrashim, as well as *Otzar Yisrael*, *Otzar Dinim u-Minhagim*, and other classic rabbinic reference works.
