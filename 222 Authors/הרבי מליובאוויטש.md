# הרבי מליובאוויטש

## Background Info
Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson (1902–1994 / תרס"ב–תשנ"ד), seventh Rebbe of Chabad-Lubavitch (חב"ד-ליובאוויטש). Global Jewish spiritual leader and prolific author across revealed and esoteric Torah scholarship (*nigleh* and *nistar*), including *Likkutei Sichot* and extensive responsa.
