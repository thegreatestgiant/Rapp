# רבי צבי אלימלך מדינוב

## Background Info
Rabbi Tzvi Elimelech Shapira of Dinov (הרה"ק רבי צבי אלימלך מדינוב, 1783–1841 / ה'תקמ"ג–ה'תר"א), prominent Hasidic master, kabbalist, and halakhic authority. Universally renowned as the **Bnei Yissaschar** (*בני יששכר*) after his classic work on the festivals, months of the Jewish calendar, and Shabbat. Disciple of the Chozeh of Lublin and the Maggid of Kozhnitz. Also authored *Agra D'Kallah*, *Maggid Ta'alumah*, and *Derech Pikudecha*.
