# הרב יצחק יוסף

## Background Info
Rabbi Yitzchak Yosef (born 1952), former Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Israel (Rishon LeZion), author of the comprehensive halachic work *Yalkut Yosef* based on the rulings of his father, Maran Rav Ovadia Yosef zt"l.
