# הנצי''ב

**Rabbi Naftali Zvi Yehuda Berlin** (1816 – 1893), widely known by the acronym **Netziv** (נצי"ב), was one of the greatest rabbis of 19th-century European Jewry. He served as the Rosh Yeshiva (dean) of the famous Volozhin Yeshiva for nearly forty years, guiding it through its most flourishing period as well as its eventual closure by the Russian government in 1892.

### Background
- **Volozhin Yeshiva**: Under his leadership, Volozhin grew to become the flagship institution of the Lithuanian yeshiva world. He fought fiercely to maintain its traditional curriculum and spiritual independence against the demands of the Russian authorities.
- **Approach to Torah**: The Netziv was unique among his contemporaries for his profound emphasis on the deep study of Chumash (the Five Books of Moses) and the Prophets, as well as the Talmudic works of the Geonim and early Rishonim.
- **Chovevei Zion**: He was a prominent early supporter of the *Chovevei Zion* (Lovers of Zion) movement, advocating strongly for the agricultural settlement of the Land of Israel by religious Jews.

### Major Works
- **Ha'amek Davar** (העמק דבר): His magnum opus, a deeply original and widely studied commentary on the Torah.
- **Meishiv Davar** (משיב דבר): His primary collection of Halachic responsa.
- **Ha'amek She'ela** (העמק שאלה): A seminal commentary on the *She'iltot* of Rav Achai Gaon.
- **Meromei Sadeh** (מרומי שדה): His novellae on the Talmud.
