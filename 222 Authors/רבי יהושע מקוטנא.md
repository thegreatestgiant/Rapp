# רבי יהושע מקוטנא

## Background Info
Rabbi Yehoshua Trunk of Kutno (רבי יהושע טרונק מקוטנא, 1820–1893 / ה'תקפ"א–ה'תרנ"ג), leading 19th-century Polish halachic authority, Rav of Kutno (קוטנא), and early supporter of Chovevei Zion (חיבת ציון). Renowned for his responsa work **Yeshu'ot Malko** (**ישועות מלכו**, on Rambam and the four parts of Shulchan Aruch) and his novellae **Yavin Shemu'ah** (**יבין שמועה**).
