# חזקוני

## Background Info
Rabbi Hezekiah ben Manoah (חזקיה בן מנוח, 13th century, France / צרפת), prominent medieval French biblical commentator. Renowned for his classic commentary on the Torah, **Chizkuni** (**חזקוני**), in which he synthesized and distilled dozens of Rishonic commentaries (including Rashi, Rashbam, Ibn Ezra, Bekhor Shor, and Midrashim) alongside his own original explanations.
