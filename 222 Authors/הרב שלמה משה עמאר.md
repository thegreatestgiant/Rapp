# הרב שלמה משה עמאר

## Background Info
Rabbi Shlomo Moshe Amar (born 1948), celebrated Sephardic posek who served as the Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Israel (Rishon LeZion) from 2003 to 2013 and subsequently as the Sephardic Chief Rabbi of Jerusalem. Renowned for his vast mastery across all four sections of the Shulchan Aruch and his leadership in complex contemporary halachic issues (including agunot, medical ethics, and personal status). He is the author of the acclaimed multi-volume responsa series *Shema Shlomo* (שו"ת שמע שלמה).
