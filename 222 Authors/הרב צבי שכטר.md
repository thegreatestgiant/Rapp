# הרב צבי שכטר

## Background Info
Rabbi Hershel (Tzvi) Schachter (הרב צבי שכטר, born 1938 / ה'תרצ"ח), prominent contemporary American posek, Rosh Yeshiva and Rosh Kollel at Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Theological Seminary (RIETS) at Yeshiva University, and Senior Halakhic Consultant for OU Kosher. Renowned disciple of Rabbi Joseph B. Soloveitchik (הגרי"ד סולובייצ'יק). Author of foundational works in Halacha and Jewish thought, including *Ginat Egoz* (*גינת אגוז*), *Nefesh HaRav* (*נפש הרב*), *Eretz HaTzvi* (*ארץ הצבי*), and *Be'ikvei HaTzon* (*בעקבי הצאן*).
