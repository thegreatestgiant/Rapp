# רבי שמריהו שולמן

## Background Info
Rav Shmaryahu Shulman (1923–2021), also known as Shmerel, was a prominent talmid chacham and author who was one of the last remaining members of the Agudas Harabbonim from the 1940s and 50s. He was a leading talmid of Rav Yaakov Yitzchok Ruderman and Rav Aharon Kotler, receiving semicha from Rav Ruderman and learning at Yeshivas Ner Yisroel and Bais Medrash Govoah in Lakewood. Author of *Ohr LiYesharim*, *Be'er Sarim*, *Me'irat Einayim*, *Mareh Kohen*, and *Shomer HaPetach*.

_He was a stock broker, and the Rebbe of Rabbi Schachter._
