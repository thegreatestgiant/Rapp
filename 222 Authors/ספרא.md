# ספרא

## Background Info
The **Sifra** (**ספרא**, Aramaic for "The Book", also known as **Torat Kohanim** / **תורת כהנים**) is the premier Tannaitic Halakhic Midrash on the Book of Vayikra (Leviticus), compiled in the Land of Israel primarily following the academy and traditions of Rabbi Akiva. It forms a cornerstone of halakhic analysis, methodically demonstrating how the Oral Torah's laws derive directly from the Scriptural text.
