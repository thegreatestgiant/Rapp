# רבי יצחק עראמה

## Background Info
**Rabbi Yitzchak ben Moshe Arama** (c. 1420–1494), known as **בעל העקידה** (author of the *Akeidah*), was a Spanish rabbi, philosopher, and preacher. His masterwork, ***Akeidat Yitzchak*** (עקידת יצחק, "The Binding of Isaac"), is a profound philosophical-homiletical commentary on the Torah and the Five Megillot divided into 105 *she'arim* (gates). Each gate consists of a homiletical discourse (*derush*) followed by a textual commentary (*perush*), synthesizing Aristotelian philosophy, Maimonidean thought, and traditional rabbinic exegesis.
