# רבי מנשה קליין

## Background Info
Rabbi Menashe Klein (רבי מנשה קליין, 1924–2011), the Ungvarer Rav and Rosh Yeshiva of Beis She'arim in Brooklyn and Jerusalem. Prolific posek and author of the comprehensive 18-volume responsa series *She'elot U'Tshuvot Mishneh Halachot* (שו"ת משנה הלכות), addressing complex modern halachic questions across all four sections of Shulchan Aruch.
