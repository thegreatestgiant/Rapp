# יצחק אלחנן ספקטור

## Background Info
Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spektor (רבי יצחק אלחנן ספקטור, 1817–1896 / ה'תקע"ז–ה'תרנ"ו), revered Rav of Kovno (Kaunas) and paramount halakhic arbiter of 19th-century Ashkenazic Jewry. Renowned internationally for his monumental responsa collections *Be'er Yitzchak* (**שו"ת באר יצחק**), *Ein Yitzchak* (**שו"ת עין יצחק**), and his novellae on *Choshen Mishpat*, *Nachal Yitzchak* (**נחל יצחק**). He was celebrated for his immense scholarship, leadership of Lithuanian Jewry, and deep sensitivity and compassion in resolving complex *agunah* cases.
