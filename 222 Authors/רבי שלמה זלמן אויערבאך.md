# רבי שלמה זלמן אויערבאך

## Background Info
Rabbi Shlomo Zalman Auerbach (רבי שלמה זלמן אויערבאך, 1910–1995 / תר"ע–תשנ"ה), Rosh Yeshiva of Kol Torah (קול תורה) in Jerusalem and one of the preeminent halachic authorities of the 20th century. Renowned for groundbreaking rulings on contemporary issues such as medical ethics, electricity on Shabbat, and agricultural commandments in Eretz Yisrael. Author of responsa **Minchat Shlomo** (**שו"ת מנחת שלמה**), **Ma'adanei Eretz** (**מעדני ארץ**), and numerous halachic treatises.
