# רבי שמואל הלוי

## Background Info
Rabbi Shmuel ben David Moshe HaLevi (רבי שמואל הלוי, c. 1625–1681 / ה'שפ"ה–ה'תמ"א), prominent Polish-Lithuanian Acharon, Av Beis Din of Meseritz and Novogrudok. Renowned as the **Baal Nachalat Shiva** after his classical halachic work *Nachalat Shiva* (**נחלת שבעה**) on legal contracts, marriage, gittin, and monetary halacha, as well as his responsa collection **שו"ת נחלת שבעה**.
