# חיד''א

## Background Info
Rabbi Chaim Yosef David Azulai (the Chida, 1724–1806), renowned Sephardic posek, kabbalist, bibliographer, and prolific author of works including *Petach Einayim*, *Chaim Sha'al*, and *Shem HaGedolim*.
