# רבי שלום מרדכי שוודרון

## Background Info
Rabbi Shalom Mordechai Schwadron (1835–1911), widely known by his acronym Maharsham (an abbreviation of Moreinu HaRav Shalom Mordechai), was a preeminent halachic authority and one of the most influential Torah scholars of the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Known as the "Gaon of Berezhany", he authored several seminal works including the *Shailos Uteshuvos Maharsham* (over 3,700 responsa), *Daas Torah*, *Mishpat Shalom*, and *Techeiles Mordechai*. He is the grandfather of the famous "Maggid of Jerusalem", Rabbi Sholom Schwadron.
