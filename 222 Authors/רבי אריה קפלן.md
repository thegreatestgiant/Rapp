# רבי אריה קפלן

## Background Info
Rabbi Aryeh Kaplan (רבי אריה קפלן, 1934–1983), American Orthodox physicist, thinker, and prolific author. Renowned for his lucid translations and expositions of Jewish thought, Kabbalah, Chassidut, and his milestone Chumash translation *The Living Torah*.
