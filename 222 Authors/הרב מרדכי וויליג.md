# הרב מרדכי וויליג

## Background Info
Rabbi Mordechai Willig (הרב מרדכי וויליג, born 1947 / ה'תש"ז), Rosh Yeshiva and Rosh Kollel at Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Theological Seminary (RIETS) of Yeshiva University, Av Beit Din of the Beth Din of America (בית הדין דאמריקה), and Rabbi of the Young Israel of Riverdale in New York. Author of the halachic series *Am Mordechai* (*עם מרדכי*) and numerous halachic essays in the journal *Kol Tzvi* (*קול צבי*).
