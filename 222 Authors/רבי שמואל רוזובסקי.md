# רבי שמואל רוזובסקי

## Background Info
- **Lifespan**: 1913–1979 (תרע"ג–תשל"ט)
- **Role**: Legendary Rosh Yeshiva of the Ponevezh Yeshiva in Bnei Brak. Disciple of the Grodno Yeshiva (under Rabbi Shimon Shkop) and the Mir Yeshiva (under Rabbi Yerucham Levovitz).
- **Works**: Author of *Kuntresei Shiurim* (*קונטרסי שעורים*) and *Shiurei Reb Shmuel* on Nashim and Nezikin, renowned throughout the yeshiva world for methodological structure, penetrating questions, and foundational yeshivishe derech halimud.
