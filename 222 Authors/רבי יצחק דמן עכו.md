# רבי יצחק דמן עכו

## Background Info
Rabbi Isaac of Acre (רבי יצחק דמן עכו, 13th–14th century), prominent Kabbalist of the Rishonim and disciple of the Ramban (רמב"ן). Author of the kabbalistic diary and commentary **Otzar HaChaim** (**אוצר החיים**) and **Me'irat Einayim** (**מאירת עיניים**).
