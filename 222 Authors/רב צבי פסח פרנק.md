# רב צבי פסח פרנק

## Background Info
Rabbi Tzvi Pesach Frank (הרב צבי פסח פרנק, 1873–1960 / תרל"ג–תשכ"א), Chief Rabbi of Jerusalem (ירושלים) for decades and one of the 20th century's leading halachic decisors. Author of the renowned responsa series *Har Tzvi* (שו"ת הר צבי across all four parts of the Shulchan Aruch), *Mikdash David* (מקדש דוד), and extensive novellae.
