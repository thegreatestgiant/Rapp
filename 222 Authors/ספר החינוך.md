# ספר החינוך

## Background Info
**Sefer HaChinuch** (**ספר החינוך**, "The Book of Education") is a classic 13th-century rabbinic work authored anonymously in Barcelona (conventionally attributed to Rabbi Aharon HaLevi of Barcelona, the Ra'ah, or his brother Rabbi Pinchas HaLevi). It systematically enumerates and expounds upon the 613 Mitzvot in the order of the weekly Torah portions, articulating for each commandment its root rationale (*shoresh ha-mitzvah*), foundational halakhic parameters, and application across generations.
