# הרב בן ציון פירר

## Background Info
Rabbi Ben Tzion Firer (1914–1988), rabbi of Nir Galim, renowned Torah scholar, ethicist, and prolific author of *Panim Chadashot BaTorah* (פנים חדשות בתורה) on the Chumash, among other works.
