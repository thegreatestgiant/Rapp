# שולחן ערוך הרב

## Background Info
Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi (רבי שניאור זלמן מלאדי, 1745–1812 / ה'תק"ה–ה'תקע"ג), founder of Chabad Hasidism (חסידות חב"ד), known as the **Alter Rebbe** (אדמו"ר הזקן). Author of the foundational philosophical work **Tanya** (**ספר התניא**) and **Shulchan Aruch HaRav** (**שולחן ערוך הרב** / Shulchan Aruch of the Alter Rebbe), composed at the instruction of his teacher the Maggid of Mezeritch as a lucid and reasoned halachic code.
