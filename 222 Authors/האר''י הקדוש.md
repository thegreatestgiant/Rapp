# האר''י הקדוש

## Background Info
Rabbi Isaac Luria Ashkenazi (רבי יצחק לוריא בן שלמה אשכנזי, 1534–1572 / ה'רצ"ד–ה'של"ב), universally revered as the **Arizal** (האר''י הקדוש / אריז''ל). Foremost mystic and kabbalist of the modern era, whose revolutionary kabbalistic system ("Lurianic Kabbalah") transformed Jewish thought, liturgy, and mysticism. His teachings were transcribed and recorded primarily by his chief disciple, Rabbi Chaim Vital (*Kitvei Ha'Ari*), including foundational works such as *Etz Chaim*, *Pri Etz Chaim*, and *Sefer HaLikutim* (*ספר הליקוטים* - kabbalistic expositions and homilies on Tanach).
