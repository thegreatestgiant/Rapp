# הרב נחום לאם

## Background Info
Rabbi Dr. Norman Lamm (הרב ד"ר נחום לאם, 1927–2020 / ה'תרפ"ח–ה'תש"ף), leading American Jewish educator, thinker, and community leader; third president and Rosh Yeshiva of Yeshiva University (YU). Close disciple of Rabbi Joseph B. Soloveitchik (הגרי"ד סולובייצ'יק). Author of numerous books and studies in Halacha, Jewish thought, and the history of Hasidism, including *Torah Lishmah* (*תורה לשמה*) and *Hahigayon BeHalacha* (*ההגיון בהלכה*).
