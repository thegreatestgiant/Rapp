# רבי יצחק הוטנר

## Background Info
Rabbi Yitzchok Hutner (רבי יצחק הוטנר, 1906–1980), renowned Rosh Yeshiva of Yeshiva Rabbi Chaim Berlin in Brooklyn, New York, and member of the Moetzes Gedolei HaTorah. Master of Jewish thought and Talmudic analysis, celebrated for his monumental multi-volume philosophical and homiletical work *Pachad Yitzchok* (פחד יצחק) on the Jewish holidays and shabbat.
