# הרב אברהם יצחק הכהן קוק

## Background Info
Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak HaKohen Kook (1865–1935), known by his acronym the Ra'ayah (הראי"ה). The first Ashkenazi Chief Rabbi of the Land of Israel and founder of both the Chief Rabbinate of Israel and Yeshivat Mercaz HaRav. One of the preeminent halakhic decisors, kabbalists, and Jewish thinkers of modern times. Author of major responsa works including *Mishpat Kohen*, *Orach Mishpat*, *Da'at Kohen*, and *Ezrat Kohen*, along with foundational philosophical writings such as *Orot* and *Orot HaKodesh*.
