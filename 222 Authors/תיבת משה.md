# תיבת משה

## Background Info
*Teivat Moshe* (תיבת משה), an analytical halachic work focusing on the laws of Shabbat and the 39 Melachot (*Lamed-Tet Melachot*). In *Melechet Zorei'a* (מלאכת זורע), the author examines the fundamental mechanism of planting on Shabbat, discussing whether the Torah prohibition takes effect immediately at the time of the action (*she'at ma'aseh*) or depends on eventual rooting (*hashrasha*), in dialogue with the *Minchat Chinuch*, *Or Same'ach*, and the *Turei Even*.
