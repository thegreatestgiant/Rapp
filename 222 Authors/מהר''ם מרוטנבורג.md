# מהר''ם מרוטנבורג

## Background Info
Rabbi Meir ben Baruch of Rothenburg (מהר"ם מרוטנבורג, c. 1215–1293 / ה'תתקע"ה–ה'נ"ג), towering Rishon, supreme spiritual and halakhic authority of German and Ashkenazic Jewry, and primary mentor to the Rosh (Rabbi Asher ben Yechiel) and the Mordechai. His thousands of responsa (*Teshuvot Maharam MiRothenburg*, printed in classical editions including Prague, Lemberg, Cremona, and Berlin) formed the bedrock of Ashkenazic pesak and communal jurisprudence. He famously died in prison in Ensisheim after being captured while fleeing to the Land of Israel, having refused to allow the Jewish community to pay an exorbitant ransom that would encourage further kidnappings.
