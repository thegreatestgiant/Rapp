# רבי שלמה גאנצפריד

## Background Info
Rabbi Shlomo Ganzfried (רבי שלמה גאנצפריד, 1804–1886 / ה'תקס"ד–ה'תרמ"ו), prominent Hungarian posek, Rosh Beth Din of Ungvár (Uzhhorod). Universally celebrated as the author of the *Kitzur Shulchan Aruch* (*קיצור שולחן ערוך*), the classic, ubiquitous handbook of practical daily Halacha. Also authored *Apiryon*, *Lechem VeSimlah*, and *Ohalei Shem*.
