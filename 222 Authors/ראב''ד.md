# ראב''ד

## Background Info
Rabbi Avraham ben David of Posquières (רבי אברהם בן דוד מפוזקייר, **הראב"ד** / **ראב"ד השלישי**; c. 1125–1198 / ד'תתפ"ה–ד'תתקנ"ט), preeminent Provençal Rishon, rosh yeshiva, posek, and foundational Kabbalist. 

Renowned throughout rabbinic literature primarily for his incisive critical glosses, **השגות הראב"ד** (*Hasagot HaRa'avad*), printed alongside the Rambam's *Mishneh Torah* in all standard editions. He is also celebrated for his commentaries and defense of rabbinic tradition on the Rif (*ספר הזכות*), his critiques of the Ba'al HaMa'or (*כתוב שם*), his commentary on Torat Kohanim (Sifra), and his seminal halakhic treatise on hilchot niddah, **בעלי הנפש** (*Ba'alei HaNefesh*).
