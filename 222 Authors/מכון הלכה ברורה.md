# מכון הלכה ברורה

## Background Info
- **Nature**: Torani Research Institute and Publishing Project (מכון תורני).
- **Founding & Vision**: Founded based on the grand vision of Maran HaRav Avraham Yitzchak HaKohen Kook (הראי"ה קוק) to integrate Talmudic sugyot with practical Halacha, and established as an institute by his son HaRav Tzvi Yehuda Kook.
- **Works**: *Halacha Berura* (the direct halachic rulings of the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch alongside the Talmudic text, written by Rav Kook himself) and *Birur Halacha* (*בירור הלכה* - comprehensive encyclopedic monographs tracing the halachic development from Gemara through Rishonim, Acharonim, and Responsa).
