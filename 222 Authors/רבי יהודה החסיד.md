# רבי יהודה החסיד

## Background Info
Rabbi Judah ben Samuel of Regensburg (רבי יהודה החסיד, c. 1150–1217 / ד'תתק"י–ד'תתקע"ז), preeminent Rishon and spiritual leader of Chasidei Ashkenaz (חסידי אשכנז). Author of the seminal and widely influential **Sefer Chasidim** (**ספר חסידים**), which encompasses halachic, ethical, pietistic, and mystical guidance.
