# הרב אליעזר מלמד

## Background Info
Rabbi of the settlement and Rosh Yeshiva of Har Bracha (הר ברכה). Author of the widely acclaimed and comprehensive halachic series **Peninei Halakha** (**פניני הלכה**) covering all areas of the Shulchan Aruch and Jewish festivals.
