# מסכתות קטנות

## Background Info
A collection of approximately fifteen minor tractates (*Masechtot Ketanot*) from the Tannaitic and Amoraic eras not included in the standard Six Orders of the Mishnah (ששה סדרי משנה). They address specialized areas of Halacha, ethics, etiquette (*derekh eretz*), and scribal laws (Sefer Torah and Tefillin). Key tractates include *Avot de-Rabbi Natan* (אבות דרבי נתן), *Masechet Soferim* (סופרים), *Kallah* (כלה), *Kallah Rabbati* (כלה רבתי), *Derekh Eretz Rabbah & Zuta* (דרך ארץ רבה וזוטא), and *Semachot* (*Evel Rabbati* / מסכת שמחות).
