# רבי אליעזר בן יואל הלוי

## Background Info
Rabbi Eliezer ben Yoel HaLevi (רבי אליעזר בן יואל הלוי, c. 1140–1225 / ד'תתק"כ–ד'תתקצ"ה), one of the prominent Ba'alei HaTosafot and leading sages of medieval Ashkenaz. Widely known by his acronym **Ra'avyah** (**ראבי"ה**) or as the **Avi Ha'Ezri** (**אבי העזרי**) after his monumental halachic code and Talmudic commentary.
