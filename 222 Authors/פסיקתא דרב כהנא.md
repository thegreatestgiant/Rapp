# פסיקתא דרב כהנא

## Background Info
**Pesikta de-Rav Kahana** (פסיקתא דרב כהנא) is one of the earliest extant Aggadic Midrashic compilations, dating to the Amoraic period in Eretz Yisrael (c. 5th century CE). Arranged according to the annual cycle of festive and special Shabbat Torah and Haftarah readings, it contains homilies for holidays, special Sabbaths (the Four Parshiyot), and the weeks of consolation following Tisha B'Av. The classical modern critical edition was edited by Bernard Mandelbaum (1962), establishing the standard scholarly text and numbering of the homilies and supplementary appendices (*נספחים*).
