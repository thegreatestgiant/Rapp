# אוצר המדרשים

## Background Info
**Otzar Midrashim** (**אוצר מדרשים**) is a monumental two-volume anthology containing over 200 minor midrashim compiled, annotated, and published by [[רבי יהודה דוד אייזנשטיין]] in New York in 1915.
