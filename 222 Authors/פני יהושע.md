# פני יהושע

## Background Info
Rabbi Yaakov Yehoshua Falk (רבי יעקב יהושע פלק, 1680–1756 / ה'תמ"א–ה'תקט"ז), one of the greatest Acharonim. Author of the classical Talmudic commentary **Pnei Yehoshua** (**פני יהושע**) on Shas, which became a foundational text in Lithuanian and Ashkenazi yeshiva study.
