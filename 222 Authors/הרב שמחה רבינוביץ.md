# הרב שמחה רבינוביץ

## Background Info
Posek and prolific author in Jerusalem (ירושלים). Author of the acclaimed multi-volume halachic series **Piskei Teshuvot** (**פסקי תשובות**), providing comprehensive rulings and clarifications keyed to the order of the Shulchan Aruch and Mishnah Berurah, synthesizing the decisions of modern and contemporary Acharonim.
