# שמעון קרסנר

## Background Info
Author of the sefer **"Nachat Shimon"** (נחלת שמעון): a multi-volume work of halachic discussion ("משא ומתן בענינים הלכתיים המסתעפים מספר...") grounded in Chazal and the Acharonim. Each volume follows one book of Tanach — Yehoshua, Shoftim, Shmuel I, Rut and Melachim I — and was printed in Baltimore and Brooklyn (USA).

Citations in the shiur appear in the form `נחלת שמעון <book> <siman>`, e.g. `נחלת שמעון מלכים א׳ סימן ל''ד` (Melachim I, Chelek II, Siman 34).
