# רבי לוי יצחק מברדיטשוב

## Background Info
- **Lifespan**: 1740–1809 (ה'ת"ק–כ"ה בתשרי ה'תק"ע)
- **Lineage & Hasidic Leadership**: Born in Hoshakov, Galicia, to Rabbi Meir of Zamosc, tracing his lineage to prominent rabbinic families. An acclaimed Talmudic prodigy in his youth, he became a foremost disciple of the Maggid of Mezeritch (המגיד ממעזריטש, Rabbi Dov Ber) and Rabbi Shmelke of Nikolsburg. He held rabbinic posts in Rychwal, Zelechow, and Pinsk, facing sharp opposition from the Misnagdim, before becoming the Rav and spiritual leader of Berditchev (Berdychiv, ברדיטשוב, Ukraine) in 1785, where he served until his passing.
- **The "Advocate of Israel" (*Sanegoran Shel Yisrael*)**: Revered throughout Jewish history as the supreme defender of the Jewish people (סנגורן של ישראל). He was celebrated for his fervent prayers, boundless love of every Jew (*ahavat Yisrael*), and bold dialogues with the Almighty demanding divine mercy, recorded in famous prayers and folk melodies (such as his *Dudele* and *Din Torah mit Gott*).
- **Major Work**: Authored the Hasidic classic **Kedushat Levi** (**ספר קדושת לוי**, first published in Slavita in 1798 and Zholkva in 1811). The work expounds profound kabbalistic teachings through the lens of passionate worship (*devekut* and *hitlahavut*), faith, and the inner spiritual mechanics of the mitzvot, arranged around the weekly Torah portions and Jewish festivals (particularly Chanukah and Purim).
