# רבי שמעון שוואב

## Background Info
Rabbi Shimon Schwab (רבי שמעון שוואב, 1908–1995 / תרס"ח–תשנ"ה), prominent leader of German-Ashkenazic Orthodox Jewry in the United States, spiritual leader of K'hal Adath Jeshurun (קהל עדת ישורון / Breuer's) in Washington Heights, New York. Author of *Ma'ayan Beit HaSho'evah* (*מעין בית השואבה*) on the Torah and numerous essays on Torah im Derech Eretz.
