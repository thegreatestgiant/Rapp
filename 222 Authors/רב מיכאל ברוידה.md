# רב מיכאל ברוידה

## Background Info
Rabbi Michael J. Broyde (רב מיכאל ברוידה), Professor of Law at Emory University School of Law, senior fellow at the Center for the Study of Law and Religion, and former dayan in the Beth Din of America. Prolific author and scholar in Jewish law, comparative religious law, and ethics, frequently publishing in the *Journal of Halacha and Contemporary Society*.
