# רבי שמעון בן צמח דוראן

## Background Info
Rabbi Shimon ben Tzemach Duran (Rashbatz / Tashbetz, 1361–1444), major 15th-century Spanish-Algerian posek, philosopher, and author of the responsa collection *Tashbetz*.
