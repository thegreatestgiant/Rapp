# הרב בן ציון מאיר חי עוזיאל

## Background Info
Rabbi Ben-Zion Meir Hai Uziel (1880–1953), the Rishon LeTzion and first Sephardic Chief Rabbi of the State of Israel. One of the foremost halakhic authorities and communal leaders of the twentieth century. Author of the extensive responsa collection *Mishpetei Uziel*, as well as *Michmanei Uziel*, *Hegyonei Uziel*, and other works.
