# רבי רפאל יום טוב ליפמן היילפרין

## Background Info
- **Lifespan**: 1816–1879 (ה'תקע"ו–ד' בשבט ה'תרל"ט)
- **Background & Rabbinic Pulpits**: Born in Ruzhany, Belarus, to Rabbi Binyamin Heilprin (Rav of Ruzhany and Karlin). Recognized as a phenomenal Talmudic prodigy (*illui*) with encyclopedic command of Bavli, Yerushalmi, and halakhic poskim. He held rabbinic leadership positions in Karlin, Smilavichy, Svislach, and Vasilishki, before being chosen in 1859 as the Chief Rabbi of Bialystok (ביאליסטוק), one of the major Jewish hubs in Lithuania, where he guided the community with renowned piety, communal activism, and selfless defense of the downtrodden.
- **Responsa Masterwork**: Author of the acclaimed halakhic responsa collection **Oneg Yom Tov** (**שו"ת עונג יום טוב**, published in Vilna in 1880). Renowned for its analytical profundity, intellectual honesty, and definitive rulings across all four sections of Shulchan Aruch, particularly on complex marital laws (*Even HaEzer* and *agunot*), financial dispute jurisprudence (*Choshen Mishpat*), and ritual commandments.
