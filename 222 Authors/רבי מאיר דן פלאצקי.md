# רבי מאיר דן פלאצקי

## Background Info
**Rabbi Meir Dan Plotzky** (1866–1928) was a prominent Polish rabbinic authority, Rosh Yeshiva, and communal leader. He served as Rabbi of Dvohrt and Ostrów Mazowiecka, and was a foremost leader of Agudath Israel in Poland. He is renowned as the author of ***Kli Chemda*** (**כלי חמדה**), a multi-volume analytical commentary on the Torah, and ***Chemdat Yisrael*** (**חמדת ישראל**), an acclaimed halakhic masterpiece expounding on Sefer HaMitzvot, Minchat Chinuch, and primary commandment principles, featuring celebrated treatises including *Kuntres Ner Mitzvah*.
