# רבי יוסף קארו

## Background Info
Rabbi Joseph (Yosef) Karo (רבי יוסף קארו, 1488–1575 / ה'רמ"ח–ה'של"ה), known as **Maran** (מרן), **HaMechaber** (המחבר), and author of the **Shulchan Aruch** (**שולחן ערוך**), the definitive codification of Jewish law. He authored the monumental halakhic commentary **Beit Yosef** (**בית יוסף**) on the Arba'ah Turim, and **Kesef Mishneh** (**כסף משנה**), the foremost commentary on Maimonides' Mishneh Torah elucidating the Rambam's sources and defending him against the strictures of the Ra'avad.
