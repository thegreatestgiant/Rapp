# רבי חיים מוולוז'ין

## Background Info
Rabbi Chaim of Volozhin (רבי חיים מוולוז'ין, 1749–1821 / ה'תק"ט–ה'תקפ"א), foremost disciple of the Vilna Gaon (הגר"א) and founder of the legendary Volozhin Yeshiva (ישיבת עץ חיים בוולוז'ין), the "Mother of Yeshivas." His theological and philosophical magnum opus is **Nefesh HaChaim** (**נפש החיים**), articulating the cosmic significance of Torah study, prayer, and kabbalistic metaphysics in service of God.
