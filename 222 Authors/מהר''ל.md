# מהר''ל

## Background Info
Rabbi Judah Loew ben Bezalel (c. 1512/1525 – 1609 / 18 Elul 5369), widely known as the **Maharal of Prague** (*Moreinu Ha-Rav Loew*). He was one of the foremost Jewish thinkers, halakhists, and kabbalists of Central Europe, and served as the Chief Rabbi of Prague. His work **Gur Aryeh** is a fundamental and comprehensive supercommentary on Rashi's commentary on the Torah.
