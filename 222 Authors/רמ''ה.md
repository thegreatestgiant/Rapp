# רמ''ה

## Background Info
Rabbi Meir ben Todros HaLevi Abulafia (רמ''ה / הרמ''ה, c. 1170–1244 / ד'תתק"ל–ה'ד), prominent Rishon, Rosh Yeshiva, and Av Beit Din of Toledo, Spain. Renowned halakhist and Talmudic commentator. Celebrated author of *Yad Ramah* (*יד רמה*), monumental novellae on tractates Sanhedrin, Bava Batra, and Kiddushin, as well as the Masoretic treatise *Masoret Seyag LaTorah*.
