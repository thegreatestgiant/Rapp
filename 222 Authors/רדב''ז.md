# רדב''ז

## Background Info
Rabbi David ben Solomon ibn Abi Zimra (רבי דוד בן שלמה אבן אבי זמרא, 1479–1573 / ה'רל"ט–ה'של"ג), known by his acronym **Radbaz** (**רדב"ז**). Leading halachist, Kabbalist, and spiritual head of the Jewish communities of Egypt (מצרים) and Eretz Yisrael (ארץ ישראל). Renowned for his voluminous responsa collection **Responsa Radbaz** (**שו"ת רדב"ז**, spanning thousands of teshuvot) and his authoritative commentary on Rambam's *Mishneh Torah*.
