# רבינו חננאל

## Background Info
Rabbi Chananel ben Chushiel (רבינו חננאל בן חושיאל, **ר"ח**; c. 990–1053 / ד'תש"ן–ד'תתי"ג), leading early Rishon, Rosh Yeshiva in Kairouan (modern Tunisia), and son of Rabbi Chushiel (one of the "Four Captives").

Author of one of the earliest comprehensive running commentaries on the Babylonian Talmud (Seder Moed, Nashim, and Nezikin), integrating the teachings of the Babylonian Geonim with the Talmud Yerushalmi. His commentary is printed alongside the Gemara text in all standard Vilna editions and is cited extensively by Rashi and the Tosafists.
