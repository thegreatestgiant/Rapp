# אבני נזר

## Background Info
Rabbi Avraham Bornsztain of Sochatchov (1838–1910), founder of the Sochatchover Hasidic dynasty and one of the preeminent halachic authorities of Poland. Author of the classic responsa collection *Avnei Nezer* and the seminal work on Hilchot Shabbat, *Eglei Tal*. Son-in-law of Rabbi Menachem Mendel of Kotzk.
