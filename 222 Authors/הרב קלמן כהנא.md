# הרב קלמן כהנא

## Background Info
Rabbi Dr. Kalman Kahana (1910–1991), a leader of Poalei Agudat Yisrael, Member of the Knesset, signatory of the Israeli Declaration of Independence, and rabbi of Kibbutz Chofetz Chaim. A close disciple of the Chazon Ish and a prolific rabbinic scholar. Author of responsa and halakhic studies such as *Cheker VeIyun*, *Mitzvot HaAretz*, and other works.
